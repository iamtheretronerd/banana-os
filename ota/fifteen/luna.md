# BananaOS 15.1 — 20261118-2127

BlackBerry KEY2 LE (`luna`)

## Changes
- BB10 gestures on the real app: swiping into the Hub pushes the app aside under your finger, swiping back brings the app back, Recents shrinks the live app into its Active Frame, and holding Recents peeks with no flicker. Gesture navigation (Settings > System > Gestures) is now BB10 style: swipe up to minimise, swipe up and hold to Peek, keep going right for the Hub, swipe in from the left edge for Back; the touch keys are off in that mode. Hub: BlackBerry Hub spacing, pin chats to the top, every open app in Active Frames, the page dots back on the Phone/Camera row. Call screens follow the dark theme and the Phone app is true black. Lock screen: one battery, BB10 PIN screen. The pull-down dims the screen behind it. New Cancel icon on the power screen. Settings pages no longer draw under the status bar, and BananaOS settings are under System. Removed the extra FM app and the old Gallery; Play Store keeps its own icon
