# BananaOS 15.1 — 20261119-0344

BlackBerry KEY2 LE (`luna`)

## Changes
- Fixes for the gestures from the last update: the Recents key, holding Recents for Peek and the Hub from apps work every time again (the launcher lost its link to the system recents). Hub from an app: the app is pushed aside under your finger, and Back or swiping the Hub away takes you back to that app. Gesture navigation (Settings > System > Gestures): swipe up from the bottom to minimise, swipe up and hold to Peek, then slide right for the Hub, swipe in from the left edge for Back. The touch keys switch off only while the gestures are running, and keep working over the lock screen
