# BananaOS 15.1 — 20261120-1905

BlackBerry KEY2 LE (`luna`)

## Changes
- BB10 Calculator replaces Google Calculator: Calculator, Tip and Converter tabs, pull down the display for your history, swipe the keypad for scientific keys. Hub Peek: the Hub now lies under the wallpaper, and sliding right moves the wallpaper with the app (and its notification column) off the Hub, as on BB10. Phone: contact pictures show again in Calls, Contacts and speed dial; the call screen picture is in place from the first moment and fades in when found. Settings: the back arrow at the top is gone for good; the BananaOS page uses BB10 icons like the main page. Clock: the pull-down menu is no longer cut off, and Bedside Mode opens BananaOS Bedside Mode. Leftover apps from older builds (a second copy of the Clock, AOSP Messaging) are removed.
